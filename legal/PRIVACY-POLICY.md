# Scrollia Privacy Policy

The public, canonical privacy policy for Scrollia and Scrollia for Android is:

**https://scrollia.org/privacy/**

It is available in Spanish and English and describes the data stored on the
device, catalog requests and technical logs, Cloudflare, user-initiated Google
Translate requests, retention, deletion, and the privacy contact.

Privacy contact: **contact@scrollia.org**

Effective date: **2026-10-27**

Any change to data collection, SDKs, server logging, backups, accounts, or
third-party processing must be reflected in the public policy and in the Play
Console Data Safety declaration before release.
